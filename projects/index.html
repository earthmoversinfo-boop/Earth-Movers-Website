Site Record

The work, photographed on site.

Every photograph here was taken on an Earth Movers job — no stock, no renders. Select any frame to see it full size.

Road base, asphalt and stone-pitched drainage

Fujairah

An access road built through the mountain plant area — subgrade tested before it was covered, aggregate base spread and trimmed to level, the wearing course laid by paver and rolled, and the run-off channels beside it pitched by hand in graded rock.

Resurfacing, patch repairs and road markings

Fujairah

A live road rebuilt without closing it: failed sections saw-cut, dug out and reinstated, a full-width overlay laid and compacted by a paver train, and the crossings re-marked at the end of the shift before traffic came back on.

Kerb, channel and chamber works

Street-level infrastructure on a live urban road — kerb lines lifted and reset to new radii, sub-base regraded and rolled behind them, and utility chambers cut out, rebuilt and brought back up to the finished surface level.

Footpath and interlock paving

Footpaths and public areas relaid in interlock — bedding sand screeded to level, blocks laid to the pattern, edges cut in against the kerb line, and the surface compacted and jointed before it was handed back.
