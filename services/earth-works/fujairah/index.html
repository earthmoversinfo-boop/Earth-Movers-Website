Local coverage

Earth Works in Fujairah, delivered with our own fleet.

Request a quote

We have delivered road and asphalt packages on the east coast since 2021, including works for Fujairah Cement Industry at Dibba. Mountain terrain, port traffic and long haulage distances are planned into the programme rather than discovered on site.

Everything that happens before a structure or a road can be built: cutting, filling, levelling and compacting raw ground into a tested, build-ready platform. We own the fleet — excavators, dozers, loaders, graders, rollers and rock breakers — so programme is set by the works, not by hire availability.

Areas we cover in Fujairah: Dibba, Masafi, Fujairah Port and Free Zone, Qidfa and the Kalba road corridor. Whether the scope is a single plot or a multi-phase infrastructure package, the same plant, operators and supervision deliver it.

Our earth works services

What we deliver in Fujairah.

Excavation in Fujairah

Bulk and detailed excavation for basements, foundations, services and infrastructure corridors — from open cut in sand to hard strata broken out with hydraulic breakers. Batters, benching and shoring are planned to the geotechnical report, and arisings are hauled and disposed of under permit.

More on excavation

Back Filling in Fujairah

Structural and service backfill placed in controlled layers with approved material, watered and compacted to the specified density. Trench and basement surrounds are the classic source of settlement later — we test each lift rather than filling to the top and hoping.

More on back filling

Cut & Fill in Fujairah

Balanced cut-and-fill operations that bring a site to design levels while keeping material on site wherever it is suitable. Survey-controlled with machine guidance, so haulage, import and disposal costs are cut without losing the design profile.

More on cut & fill

Leveling & Compaction in Fujairah

Fine levelling to design elevations and falls, then layer-by-layer compaction with the right roller for the material — supported by field density testing and compaction certificates that satisfy consultant and authority sign-off.

More on leveling & compaction

Site Preparation & Land Clearing in Fujairah

Clearing a plot down to workable ground: vegetation and root removal, demolition arisings, buried slabs, abandoned services and uncontrolled fill taken out and disposed of under permit, then the platform graded, stabilised and handed over ready to build.

More on site preparation & land clearing
Common questions

Earth Works in Fujairah — FAQs

Do you carry out earth works in Fujairah?

Yes. We deliver earth works across Fujairah, including Dibba, Masafi, Fujairah Port and Free Zone, Qidfa and the Kalba road corridor, working to Fujairah Municipality standards with our own excavators, dozers, graders and rollers.

Are you an approved contractor for works in Fujairah?

We are an RTA-approved contractor and work to the requirements of Fujairah Municipality. Method statements, permits and material approvals are prepared and submitted by our own team.

How do I get a price for excavation in Fujairah?

Send drawings, a bill of quantities or a description of the scope to info@earthmoversint.com, or call +971 55 172 7024. Where it helps, we walk the ground with you before pricing.

How quickly can you mobilise to Fujairah?

Because the fleet is owned rather than hired, mobilisation is a scheduling question rather than an availability one — typically within days of approval.
