Local coverage

Road Works in Ras Al Khaimah, delivered with our own fleet.

Request a quote

RAK ground is rock as often as it is sand. Our rock breakers, high-reach machines and quarry-experienced operators handle the mountain and hard-strata work that standard excavation fleets stall on, under RAK Municipality approvals.

Full road packages from formation to final surface: sub-base and road base, asphalt binder and wearing courses, interlock, kerbs, parking and markings. As an RTA-approved road contractor we build to authority specification, with material approvals and testing documented as we go.

Areas we cover in Ras Al Khaimah: Al Ghail, Al Hamra, RAK Industrial Zones, Digdaga, Khuzam and the Al Jazeera corridor. Whether the scope is a single plot or a multi-phase infrastructure package, the same plant, operators and supervision deliver it.

What we deliver in Ras Al Khaimah.

Access Roads in Ras Al Khaimah

Temporary and permanent access roads into plots, sites, plants and remote facilities — formation, sub-base, road base and surfacing, sized for the loads that will actually use them. Built early so heavy deliveries are not stuck at the boundary.

More on access roads

Road Base Laying in Ras Al Khaimah

Supply, spreading, watering and compaction of aggregate sub-base and road base to specified thickness and density. Levels are surveyed and layers are tested before anything is paved over — the layer that decides whether a road lasts.

More on road base laying

Asphalt Works in Ras Al Khaimah

Binder and wearing course asphalt laid by paver to specified thickness, temperature and joint detail, with approved mixes from certified plants. Includes tack coat, prime coat, milling of existing surfaces and rolling to the required density.

More on asphalt works

Road Maintenance in Ras Al Khaimah

Planned and reactive maintenance for internal roads, industrial estates and community networks: resurfacing, crack sealing, edge repair, drainage clearing and reinstatement — programmed to keep the network open while work proceeds.

More on road maintenance

Asphalt Patch Works in Ras Al Khaimah

Saw-cut, excavate, reinstate and compact — patch repairs done properly so the joint does not fail in the first season. Ideal after utility crossings, trench reinstatement, or pothole and settlement repair across car parks and estate roads.

More on asphalt patch works

Parking Construction in Ras Al Khaimah

Complete parking areas from sub-grade to line marking: base, surfacing in asphalt or interlock, kerbing, drainage falls, wheel stops, bay marking and signage — built to authority layout standards and handed over ready to use.

More on parking construction

Heavy Duty Interlock Paving in Ras Al Khaimah

Heavy-duty interlock and paver block for yards, container areas, service roads and hardstandings that see loaded trucks and forklifts. Correct bedding sand, edge restraint and block thickness — the difference between a yard that holds and one that ruts.

More on heavy duty interlock paving

Kerbstones in Ras Al Khaimah

Supply and installation of kerbstones and channels to line and level on concrete haunching — road kerbs, parking kerbs, radius units and drop kerbs at crossings and entrances, finished to authority profile.

More on kerbstones

Road Markings in Ras Al Khaimah

Thermoplastic and cold-paint road markings: lane lines, hatching, arrows, crossings, parking bays, numbering and rumble strips — applied to authority standards with reflective beading where specified.

Common questions

Road Works in Ras Al Khaimah — FAQs

Do you carry out road works in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes. We deliver road works across Ras Al Khaimah, including Al Ghail, Al Hamra, RAK Industrial Zones, Digdaga, Khuzam and the Al Jazeera corridor, working to RAK Municipality and the Public Works Department standards with our own excavators, dozers, graders and rollers.

Are you an approved contractor for works in Ras Al Khaimah?

We are an RTA-approved contractor and work to the requirements of RAK Municipality and the Public Works Department. Method statements, permits and material approvals are prepared and submitted by our own team.

How do I get a price for access roads in Ras Al Khaimah?

Send drawings, a bill of quantities or a description of the scope to info@earthmoversint.com, or call +971 55 172 7024. Where it helps, we walk the ground with you before pricing.

How quickly can you mobilise to Ras Al Khaimah?

Because the fleet is owned rather than hired, mobilisation is a scheduling question rather than an availability one — typically within days of approval.
